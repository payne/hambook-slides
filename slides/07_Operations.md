# **Chapter 7: Operating Practices**
### How to Act on the Air

---

## **Section 7.1: FM Operation (VHF/UHF)**

### Simplex
* Direct communication (Radio to Radio).
* **National Calling Frequency:**
  * **2m:** 146.520 MHz
  * **70cm:** 446.000 MHz
* Use this to find a contact, then move to another frequency to chat.

### Initiating a Call
* **On Repeaters:** Just say your call sign.
  * *"N3PAY monitoring"* or *"N3PAY listening."*
  * **Do NOT** call "CQ" on a repeater (it shows you are a newbie).
* **Breaking In:** Wait for a pause and say your call sign.
* **Emergency:** Say "Break Break" or "Emergency."

---

## **Section 7.2: HF Operation**

### Calling CQ
* **Meaning:** "I am calling any station."
* **Format:** "CQ CQ CQ, this is [Your Call Sign], [Your Call Sign], [Your Call Sign], calling CQ and standing by."

### Q-Codes (Shorthand)
* **QRM:** Man-made interference (Jammers/Noise).
* **QRN:** Natural interference (Static/Lightning).
* **QSY:** Changing frequency.
* **QTH:** Location.
* **QSL:** I acknowledge / Can you confirm receipt?

## **Band Plans**

### The Gentleman's Agreement
* The FCC tells us **where** we can transmit (Privileges).
* The Community decides **what** goes where (Band Plan).
* **Example:**
  * 144.0 - 144.1 MHz: CW Only (Weak Signal).
  * 146.0 - 148.0 MHz: FM Repeaters.
* *Always listen before you transmit!*

## **Section 7.3: Public Service**

### Traffic Handling
* **Formal Traffic:** Messages sent on behalf of third parties.
* **Preamble:** The header of the message (Check count, Origin, Date).
* **Phonetic Alphabet:** Use standard NATO phonetics (Alpha, Bravo, Charlie) to spell out difficult words.
  * *Don't use "Cute" phonetics (e.g., Nancy Three Paul Always Yelling) for formal traffic.*

### RACES vs ARES
* **RACES:** Registered with Civil Defense (Govt activated).
* **ARES:** Amateur Radio Emergency Service (ARRL sponsored).

---

## **Section 7.4: Satellite Operation**

### Space Radio
* Satellites are "Repeaters in the Sky" (LEO - Low Earth Orbit).
* **Uplink/Downlink:** Usually on different bands (e.g., Uplink on 2m, Downlink on 70cm).

### Doppler Shift
* As the satellite moves **toward** you, the frequency goes **UP**.
* As it moves **away**, the frequency goes **DOWN**.
* You must constantly tune your radio to compensate.

## **Section 7.5: Fun Stuff**

### Contesting
* The goal: Contact as many people as possible in a set time.
* Exchange: Usually minimal info (Call sign + Signal Report).

### Fox Hunting
* **Radio Direction Finding (RDF):** locating a hidden transmitter.
* Used for fun (contests) or serious work (finding a stuck microphone or jammer).
