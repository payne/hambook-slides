* Also known as **"Ham Radio"**

### The Foundation of Radio

## **Section 1.1: Electrical Basics**

### The Water Analogy

* Electricity behaves like water flowing through a pipe
* **Voltage (E):** The pressure pushing the water (*Potential*)
* **Current (I):** The volume of water flowing (*Flow*)
* **Resistance (R):** The restriction in the pipe (*Opposition*)

---

## **Section 1.2: Units & Quantities**

### The Language of Electronics

| Term | Symbol | Unit | Unit Abbr. |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: |
| **Voltage** | **E** (or V) | Volts | V |
| **Current** | **I** | Amperes | A |
| **Resistance** | **R** | Ohms | $\Omega$ |
| **Power** | **P** | Watts | W |

---

## **Section 1.3: Ohm’s Law**

### The Magic Triangle

* Describes the relationship between Voltage, Current, and Resistance
* **Formulas:**
  * $E = I \times R$ *(Voltage = Current × Resistance)*
  * $I = E \div R$ *(Current = Voltage ÷ Resistance)*
  * $R = E \div I$ *(Resistance = Voltage ÷ Current)*

**Practice Question:**
*If you have a 12-volt battery and a 6-ohm light bulb, how much current flows?*
**(Answer: 2 Amps)**

---

## **Power Calculations**

### Calculating Energy Usage

* **Power (P):** The rate at which energy is used (heat or RF energy)
* **Formula:**
  * $P = I \times E$ *(Power = Current × Voltage)*
  * *Remember "PIE"*

**Example:**
* A handheld radio uses **12 Volts** and draws **2 Amps**
* $P = 2 \text{A} \times 12 \text{V} = \mathbf{24 \text{ Watts}}$

## **Chapter 1 Review**

### Quick Recap

* **Voltage** is pressure (Volts)
* **Current** is flow (Amps)
* **Resistance** opposes flow (Ohms)
* **Ohm's Law:** $E = I \times R$
* **Power:** $P = I \times E$

**Class Challenge:**
*I have 10 Volts and 2 Amps... what is the Resistance?*
