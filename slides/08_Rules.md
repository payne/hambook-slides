# **Chapter 8: Rules and Regulations**
### Keeping It Legal

---

## **Section 8.1: Licenses & Call Signs**

### The Three Current Classes
1.  **Technician:** Entry level. VHF/UHF + Limited HF.
2.  **General:** The "World" license. Access to most HF bands.
3.  **Amateur Extra:** The top tier. Access to ALL amateur frequencies.

### Call Signs
* **Format:** Prefix (K, W, N, A) + Number (0-9) + Suffix (1-3 letters).
* **ITU Prefix:** The US uses **K, W, N,** and **AA-AL**.
* **Vanity Call Signs:** You can request a specific available call sign after you get your license.
* **Term:** Licenses are good for **10 years**. You have a 2-year grace period to renew (but you can't transmit during the grace period!).

---

## **Section 8.2: ITU Regions**

### The World Map
* The International Telecommunication Union (ITU) divides the world into 3 regions.
* **Region 1:** Europe, Africa, Northern Asia.
* **Region 2:** **North & South America** (This is us!).
* **Region 3:** Southern Asia, Australia, Pacific.
* *Note:* Frequency allocations can change depending on which region you are in.



---

## **Section 8.3: Authorized Frequencies**

### Privileges
* **VHF/UHF:** Technicians have **FULL** privileges on all amateur bands above 50 MHz (6m, 2m, 1.25m, 70cm, etc.).
* **HF (High Frequency):**
    * **10 Meters:** Voice, CW, Data (28.000 – 28.500 MHz).
    * **15, 40, 80 Meters:** CW Only.

### Primary vs. Secondary
* **Primary User:** Has priority. No interference allowed.
* **Secondary User:** Can use the band but **must not interfere** with Primary users and must accept interference from them.
* *Example:* We are secondary on parts of the 70cm band (shared with military radar).

---

## **Section 8.5: Station Identification**

### The 10-Minute Rule
* You must transmit your call sign:
    1.  **Every 10 minutes** during a conversation.
    2.  At the **end** of a conversation.
* *You do NOT need to ID at the beginning (though it is polite).*
* **Language:** English (or Morse Code).
* **Tactical Calls:** You can use names like "Event Command" or "Net Control," but you **MUST** still give your FCC call sign every 10 mins.

---

## **Section 8.6: Prohibited Transmissions**

### The "Big No-No" List
1.  **Broadcasting:** One-way transmission to the general public (Music, News).
2.  **Music:** Never allowed (except incidental from a re-broadcast space shuttle mission).
3.  **Business:** No pecuniary interest (You cannot be paid to transmit).
4.  **Encryption:** Obscuring the meaning of a message is illegal.
    * *Exception:* Control commands to a space station or drone.
5.  **Indecent/Obscene Language:** Keep it G-rated.

---

## **Section 8.7: Control Operator**

### Who is in Charge?
* **Control Operator:** The licensed ham effectively in control of the transmitter.
* **Station Licensee:** The person who owns the station call sign.
* *Often these are the same person.*
* **Responsibility:** The Control Operator is presumed responsible for proper operation.

### Types of Control
1.  **Local Control:** You are at the radio (Directly manipulating controls).
2.  **Remote Control:** You are controlling the radio over the internet/link.
3.  **Automatic Control:** The station operates without a human present (Repeaters, Beacons).
