# **Chapter 6: Station Setup**
### Putting It All Together

---

## **Section 6.1: Station Accessories**

### Essential Tools
* **Microphone:** For voice transmission.
* **Key / Paddle:** For Morse Code (CW).
* **Power Supply:** Converts 120V AC (wall) to 13.8V DC (radio).
  * *Note:* Regulated supplies prevent voltage sag.

### The Dummy Load
* **What is it?** A resistor that dissipates RF energy as heat.
* **Why use it?** To test your transmitter without sending a signal out on the air.
* **Impedance:** 50 Ohms.



---

## **Section 6.3: The Transceiver**

### Controls You Need to Know
* **VFO (Variable Frequency Oscillator):** Tuning knob to pick any frequency.
* **Memory Mode:** Jumping between saved channels.
* **Squelch:** Silences background static when no signal is present.
  * *Tip:* Turn it until the noise stops, then stop.
* **RIT (Receiver Incremental Tuning):** Changes your receive frequency *without* changing your transmit frequency.
  * Use this when the other person sounds slightly "off-frequency."

---

## **Section 6.5: Repeaters**

### Extending Your Range
* **Repeater:** A radio that listens on one frequency and simultaneously re-transmits on another with higher power/better antenna.
* **Input Frequency:** What you transmit on (Repeater listens).
* **Output Frequency:** What you listen to (Repeater transmits).



---

### Repeater Offsets
* The distance between Input and Output.
* **2-Meter Band:** usually 600 kHz (0.6 MHz).
* **70cm Band:** usually 5 MHz.
* **Shift:** You must set your radio to shift (+) or (-) when you push the PTT.

### PL Tones (CTCSS)
* **Problem:** Interference opens the repeater squelch.
* **Solution:** A sub-audible tone sent with your voice.
* The repeater only opens if it hears the correct "password" tone (e.g., 100.0 Hz).

---

## **Section 6.6: Basic Repair & Testing**

### The Multimeter
* The Swiss Army Knife of electronics.
* **Voltmeter:** Measures Voltage (Connect in parallel).
* **Ammeter:** Measures Current (Connect in series).
* **Ohmmeter:** Measures Resistance (Connect to component *without* power applied).



### Soldering
* **Solder:** A tin/lead alloy used to join wires.
* **"Cold Solder Joint":** A dull, grainy joint that makes poor electrical contact. (A good joint is shiny!).

---

## **Section 6.7: Troubleshooting**

### Common Problems
* **Hum:** Often caused by a ground loop or bad power supply filtering.
* **Distorted Audio:** You might be over-modulating (talking too loud) or your battery is low.
* **Garbled (Donald Duck) Audio:** Listening to SSB in FM mode (or vice versa).
* **Interference:** If your neighbor hears you on their TV, check your grounding and low-pass filters.
