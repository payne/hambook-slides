# **Chapter 2: Electrical Components**
### The Building Blocks of Radio

---

## **Section 2.1: Basic Components**

### 1. The Resistor
* **Function:** Restricts (limits) the flow of electrical current.
* **Analogy:** A pinch in the water pipe.
* **Unit:** Ohm ($\Omega$)
* **Variable Resistor:** Called a *Potentiometer* (e.g., volume knob).



[Image of electronic resistor symbols]


---

### 2. The Capacitor
* **Function:** Stores energy in an **Electric Field**.
* **Behavior:** Blocks DC, passes AC.
* **Unit:** Farad (F) (usually $\mu F$ or $pF$)
* **Construction:** Two conductive plates separated by an insulator (dielectric).



[Image of capacitor symbols]


---

### 3. The Inductor (Coil)
* **Function:** Stores energy in a **Magnetic Field**.
* **Behavior:** Opposes changes in current flow.
* **Unit:** Henry (H)
* **Application:** Used in filters and tuned circuits.



[Image of inductor symbols]


---

### 4. The Transformer
* **Function:** Two or more inductors sharing a magnetic field (*Mutual Inductance*).
* **Use:** Changes AC voltage levels (Step-up or Step-down).
* **Key Concept:** Does **not** work with DC.



[Image of electrical transformer symbol]


---

## **Section 2.2: Semiconductors**

### What are they?
* Materials (like Silicon) that are neither perfect conductors nor perfect insulators.
* Their conductivity can be controlled precisely.

### 1. The Diode
* **Function:** Allows current to flow in **only one direction**.
* **Analogy:** A check valve.
* **Types:**
  * **Rectifier:** Converts AC to DC.
  * **LED:** Light Emitting Diode (visual indicators).



[Image of diode symbol]


---

### 2. The Transistor
* **Function:** An electronic switch or amplifier.
* **Key Concept:** A small signal controls a larger current (*Gain*).
* **Types:**
  * **BJT (Bipolar Junction Transistor):** NPN and PNP.
  * **FET (Field Effect Transistor):** Controlled by voltage, not current.



[Image of transistor NPN PNP symbols]


---

## **Section 2.3: Other Components**

* **Batteries:** Store chemical energy to provide voltage.
* **Switches:** Mechanically open or close a circuit.
* **Relays:** Switches controlled by an electromagnet (current controls a switch).
* **Fuses:** Safety devices that melt (blow) if current is too high to protect equipment.

---

## **Section 2.4: Circuit Diagrams**

### Reading Schematics
* **Schematic:** A map of a circuit using standard symbols.
* It represents the **connection logic**, not the physical layout.
* **The Test:** You *will* see questions asking you to identify component symbols (Battery, Antenna, Transistor, etc.).



[Image of basic schematic symbols]

## **Chapter 2 Review**

### Match the Component to the Function

1. **Resistor** $\rightarrow$ Limits current
2. **Capacitor** $\rightarrow$ Stores energy in Electric Field
3. **Inductor** $\rightarrow$ Stores energy in Magnetic Field
4. **Diode** $\rightarrow$ Allows flow in one direction
5. **Transistor** $\rightarrow$ Amplifies or switches
